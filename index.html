CAB.01

Neon Snake

Steer the current. Eat cells to grow, and don't cross your own tail. Every fifth cell drops a surge pellet worth double. The walls wrap — you only lose to yourself.

CAB.02

Relay Breaker

A paddle, a ball, and a wall of charged bricks. Angle your returns off the paddle edges to reach the corners. Clear the wall to advance; each level speeds the ball and shrinks the paddle.

←→mousedrag
CAB.03

Matrix Recall

The grid flashes a sequence of nodes. Play it back in order. Each round adds one node and quickens the flash. Miss once and the run ends — how deep can you hold the pattern?

CAB.04

Reflex Gate

Wait for the gate to switch from red to ion-pink, then fire. Fire early and you fault, which adds 50 ms to your average. Five rounds are averaged into your reaction time — lower is better, and it lands on its own board.

spaceclicktap
CAB.05

Volt 2048

Slide the charge tiles together. Two equal tiles fuse into the next power. Keep merging toward the 2048 cell without filling the grid. One clean undo per run if you talk yourself into a mistake.
